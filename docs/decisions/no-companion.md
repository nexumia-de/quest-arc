# No travel companion

Issue #43 described a fellow traveller in the Valley: no needs, never
sad, only growing, the user's choice. The owner decided twice to decide
later, after the look back at eight weeks (#84). Decided on 2026-11-22
by best practice, as the owner asked for speculative "later" ideas to
be closed: **not planned**.

## Why

- **No evidence.** The [evidence review](../research.md) has no study
  that supports virtual companions for building habits; what it supports
  is feedback on what the user really did, which the build, the Valley
  and the weekly look back already give.
- **It works against graduation.** QuestArc aims to become unnecessary
  for a habit (design rules on graduation); a character that lives in
  the app gives a reason to visit the app, not to do the habit.
- **The rules it would need are exactly the risks.** Even without needs,
  a figure that rests after quiet days and stands up on return reads as
  a mood; the issue's own rules show how narrow the safe space is.
- **Room and attention.** The view has about 10 KB left in Super
  Productivity 19.3 ([decision](one-build-for-older-versions.md)), and
  every screen has one calm look (design rules); a figure on the journey
  screen and in the Valley competes with the build, which is the
  record of the user's routine.

## Consequences

- #43 is closed as not planned. The look back asks whether a companion
  is missed ([owner checks](../owner-checks.md)); a yes opens a new
  issue with this record as its starting point.
