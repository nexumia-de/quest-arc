# The Valley keeps its name; no Map and no Atlas

Issue #88 left three names for the owner: the Valley, its Map and the
Atlas. Decided by best practice on 2026-11-22, as the owner asked for
open "needs decision" points.

## Decision

- **The Valley** stays the name of the home: one path through the day
  ([journey design](../journey-design.md), #114 decision 1). It is in
  the texts already and says what the picture is.
- **No Map.** The Map was planned as a page of regions; since the
  owner's decision of 2026-11-15 the Valley itself is the home and shows
  every place, so a second overview would show the same thing twice.
- **No Atlas.** The Atlas was the planned keepsakes page. The owner
  decided on 2026-11-14 that milestones are one sentence in the weekly
  look back without a page (#87), so the name has nothing to name.

## Why

One name per thing the user sees, and no name for something that does
not exist: names that point to nothing teach users to look for it
(design rule on announcing nothing before it is earned).

## Consequences

- The roadmap's goal _The Valley_ no longer names a Map.
- If the look back asks for a keepsakes page (#84,
  [owner checks](../owner-checks.md)), its name is decided with it.
